An ongoing reflection on my development as a student and aspiring analytics professional.

Entering a new academic environment

My transition from Lotsane Senior Secondary School to Botswana Accountancy College marked an important step in my educational journey. Beginning university in 2026 meant entering a new academic environment and taking on the responsibility of pursuing a specialised field of study. As an undergraduate student in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics, I am now working towards developing knowledge and competencies that can support my future professional ambitions.

Discovering my academic direction

One of the areas that particularly interests me is big data analytics. The ability to examine large volumes of information, identify patterns and transform findings into useful insights makes this field especially appealing to me. I am also interested in business intelligence because of its role in helping organisations understand their performance and make informed decisions. These interests have strengthened my desire to develop both technical knowledge and analytical thinking.

Developing beyond the classroom

I recognise that academic development involves more than acquiring theoretical knowledge. It also requires discipline, adaptability and a willingness to engage with unfamiliar concepts. I consider these qualities important as I continue learning and improving my coding abilities. My participation in a robotics hackathon in 2025 and my experience receiving a robotics award also contributed to my interest in technology and innovation.

Looking towards the future

As I continue my studies, I intend to build a stronger foundation in data analysis and business intelligence. My immediate career interests are becoming a Data Analyst or Business Intelligence Analyst. However, my long-term ambition extends further: I aspire to become a Director of Analytics, where I can contribute to strategic decisions and lead the use of data within an organisation.

My continuing journey

I understand that I am still at the beginning of this journey. There is much more to learn, explore and experience. I want to approach my education with self-discipline, adaptability and an open mind, recognising that every stage of learning contributes to the professional I hope to become.
