01 — THE IMPORTANCE OF COMMUNICATION

Professional opportunities begin with professional communication.

The ability to communicate clearly and professionally is an important part of presenting oneself in the workplace. Whether contacting a recruiter, responding to an opportunity or submitting an application, an email should be structured, respectful and purposeful.

03 — PROFESSIONAL EMAIL ETIQUETTE

Before pressing send.

01

Use a clear subject line

Make the purpose of the email immediately understandable to the recipient.

02

Maintain professionalism

Use appropriate greetings, complete sentences and respectful language.

03

Check attachments

Ensure that the correct CV and application letter are attached before sending.

04

Proofread carefully

Review spelling, grammar, names and contact information to avoid unnecessary errors.

04 — FINAL THOUGHT

Every professional interaction is an opportunity to demonstrate clarity, discipline and intention.

A strong professional identity is communicated not only through qualifications, but also through the way one presents ideas and engages with others.
